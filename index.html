About the workshop

Bayesian Optimization (BO) is a powerful framework for the global optimization of expensive-to-evaluate black-box functions. It has become an indispensable tool across a wide range of domains, from hyperparameter tuning of machine learning models to industrial process optimization and drug discovery.

This workshop brings together researchers, engineers, and industry practitioners in a structured yet collaborative environment. It equips newcomers with a solid conceptual grounding, exposes intermediate users to diverse application domains, and provides a platform for researchers to share recent methodological developments. The workshop also aims to catalyse the formation of a Benelux-centred network of people actively working with and advancing Bayesian Optimization.

No prior knowledge of BO is required to benefit from the introductory tutorial, while the invited and contributed talks are designed to engage experts.
